Fish guide

Every spot can catch every fish, plus rare Coin and Luck Chests (stored until you Use them). Fish can roll a value variant — Silver ×1.5, Gold ×2, Diamond ×2.5, or Rainbow ×3 (only one of those) — and independently Shiny ×3, which stacks on top. Worse spots pay less (×0.7 at Creek) and make rare fish much rarer. Luck gear raises fish rarity, variants, and chest odds. Odds and pay match your current spot, gear, and active boosts.

Variants
