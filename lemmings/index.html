Level 1: just watch — they walk by themselves to the green OUT door.

Goal: save enough green walkers. Yellow IN = start. Green OUT = finish.

How to play

Lemmings

  1. Green walkers drop from the yellow IN box and walk on their own.
  2. Get enough of them into the green OUT door to clear the level.
  3. On later levels: tap a skill below, then tap a walker to use it (build bridges, dig, float, climb…).
  4. Level 1 needs no skills — press Play and wait.
